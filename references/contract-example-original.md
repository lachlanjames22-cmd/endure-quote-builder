# Contract example — original (patio contractor)

Reference only. This is the example contract Lachy supplied (2026-07-31), from a
patio/carport contractor, kept verbatim-in-substance for comparison against
`contract-terms-draft.md` (the Endure-native rewrite that went to the lawyer).

**Do not use any of this with clients.** Several clauses were deliberately NOT
carried into the Endure draft — see the notes at the bottom.

---

## Preamble
The contractor undertakes to supply and install a patio — "the works" — on the
construction site in a good, substantial, workmanlike manner in accordance with
approved plans, specifications, & the contractual terms stated below.

1. **Our main obligations** — build and complete patio(s)/carport(s)/shed(s)/
   deck(s)/roofing as described, per the contract documents. Order of
   precedence: special conditions, contract to build, standard specifications,
   quotation drawing. Right reserved to decline orders based on inaccurate
   information or errors.
2. **Exclusions** — only listed works included; prior quotes/correspondence
   excluded where inconsistent; oral communication excluded.
3. **Special conditions** — must be written on the quotation/contract document.
4. **Client warranties** — client proves ownership/permission; all permits and
   licences obtained and copies provided before work starts.
5. **Joint and several liability** — multiple owners jointly and severally
   bound; notice to one is notice to all; one signature binds all.
6. **Payment** — progress payments as arranged; breach if unpaid within
   78 days [sic — presumably 7] of demand; title retained until payment;
   contractor authorised to enter premises/vehicles "with such force as is
   necessary" and repossess goods including fixtures; client indemnifies
   contractor for the repossession.
7. **Contract price** — valid 120 days from signing; if works not started in
   that period due to client delay, contract void and costs taken from monies
   paid.
8. **Late payment** — all monies immediately due; collection charges; interest
   per Magistrates Court (Civil Proceedings) Act 2004 / Civil Judgements
   Enforcements Act 2004; solicitor and debt collector fees up to 40% of the
   amount owing; contractor may suspend works.
9. **Deposits** — refundable only if the shire rejects the application, minus
   fees incurred; forfeited if the client changes their mind after approval.
10. **Access and facilities** — uninterrupted access; power and water at
    client's cost.
11. **Time for performance** — commence within 28 days of shire approval /
    site ready / variation / progress payment; complete within 28 days of
    commencement; extensions for alterations, client delays, selections,
    access, force-majeure supply delays; notice of extension within 7 working
    days; period extends by the delay.
12. **Practical completion** — complete except omissions/defects not
    preventing reasonable use; client notified; final payment due on statement
    of practical completion.
13. **Security** — client charges the site in the contractor's favour for
    unpaid amounts and consents to an absolute caveat; title passes per stage
    payment.
14. **Surplus materials** — new materials used; surplus remains contractor's.
15. **Unforeseen conditions** — contractor not responsible for problems
    revealed during work; variation required; if client refuses, contractor
    may cancel at client's cost.
16. **Variations** — written request; contractor may decline; variation
    document with terms and cost, signed by both, before the varied work.
17. **Retention of soil** — contractor not required to remove disturbed
    sand/rock/soil.
18. **Asbestos** — no asbestos handling; licensed professional at client's
    cost.
19. **Underground services** — repairs/relocation of discovered or damaged
    sub-surface services at client's expense.
20. **Engineering** — patios engineered for single-skin/insulated roofs only;
    roof linings/solar at client's risk; steel deflection limit span/200; sag
    accepted.
21. **Posts** — no repair/refit of paving or floor materials disturbed to
    locate posts.
22. **Fascia repairs** — existing fascia repairs excluded unless stipulated.
23. **Paint** — transport/construction markings repaired with touch-up paint;
    powder-coated finish accepted.
24. **Gutters** — square patio gutters unless stated; box gutters only if
    detailed; patio built level to house — if house is out of level, ponding
    may occur and is not the contractor's liability; overflow in storms per
    NCC; class 10a non-habitable, not waterproof; spray/overflow/condensation
    non-warrantable; no liability for water damage to items under the patio.
25. **Pulldowns and electricity** — pull-down fee excludes electrical;
    client's electrician makes site safe first.
26. **Risk** — materials at client's risk once delivered.
27. **Subcontracting** — contractor may subcontract; client must not instruct
    subcontractors.
28. **Building licence** — contractor obtains licence if indicated; if
    "client obtained", client warrants accuracy; if shire rejects after
    progress payments, works deemed progressed and no refunds.
29. **Stormwater** — client directs rainwater to soak wells/drainage; nothing
    past the bottom of downpipes.
30. **Shire applications and bonds** — bonds, BAL assessments and auxiliary
    fees are the client's; only standard planning application and building
    licence included.
31. **Defects liability period** — contractor makes good defects notified in
    writing within one year of practical completion; defect = failure to
    perform in a proper and workmanlike manner / merchantable-quality
    materials; no liability for damage from work carried out by others.
32. **Warranty** — works free from defects in material and manufacture at
    sale; six (6) year structural warranty (no separation/fall/loosening from
    manufacturing defect) conditional on 6-monthly cleaning per the
    maintenance document; excludes painted finish/surface coating; long
    exclusion list (abuse, alterations, condensation, settlement, natural
    events, colour variation, radiant heat, wear and tear, third-party damage).

## Why several clauses were NOT carried into the Endure draft

- **Clause 6 repossession** ("enter with such force as is necessary", fixtures,
  client indemnifies the break-in) — unenforceable in practice, ACL risk,
  reputation poison.
- **Clause 7/9/28 forfeitures** ("contract void, costs taken from monies
  paid", deposit forfeited, "no refunds possible or permissible") — penalty
  doctrine + Home Building Contracts Act (WA) conflict.
- **Clause 8's 40% collection fee** — likely an unenforceable penalty.
- **"78 days"** — drafting error for 7; indicative of overall drafting quality.
- **6-year warranty** — contradicts Endure's 20-Year Standard; the Endure
  draft defines the 20-year structural warranty properly instead (clause 26
  of the draft).
- Roofing/gutter/fascia/engineering clauses — patio-specific; replaced with
  deck-native equivalents (timber movement, composite pass-through, footings,
  levels).
